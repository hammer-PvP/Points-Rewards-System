Earning Points

Hammer's Profile (EN) — player guide

The GM decides when to award points. Players may suggest achievements, but the GM makes the awards.

Session voting

End of Session!

  • Standout Player — 8 points: Who shone this session? Vote for the player who stood out in combat, roleplay, creativity, or a memorable moment.
  • For the Team! — 5 points: Who did something for the good of the group, even when it was not the best choice for their own character?
  • Unexpected! — 2 points: Did it go wrong? Did it go right? Either way, it was hilarious! Vote for the star of the session’s funniest disaster.

When GM tie-breaking is enabled, the GM determines the winner in a tie.
