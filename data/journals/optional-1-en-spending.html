Spending Points

Optional 1 (EN) — player guide

Redeem benefits with your earned points. The cost is recorded in your account history.

How to redeem

  1. In the Foundry right sidebar, click the Polls, Points & Rewards medal icon immediately below Chat, or use Redeem Benefit in your character sheet’s Points tab.
  2. Choose the character making the redemption.
  3. Choose a benefit and review its effect, cost, and remaining balance.
  4. For a private redemption, check Hide redemption from other players. Only you and GMs can see the Chat card.
  5. Confirm the redemption. If it was a mistake, ask the GM to use Refund on the card.

Fortuitous Encounter — further details

Fate appears to favor the character. The GM may introduce a needed item, avert a deadly situation, or arrange a meeting with a crucial NPC. This is not an automatic reward chosen by the player: it is a narrative event determined by the GM within the next ten in-game days, tied exclusively to the redeeming character’s story.

The module records points and redemptions; the GM applies gameplay and narrative effects.
