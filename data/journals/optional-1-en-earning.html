Earning Points

Optional 1 (EN) — player guide

The GM decides when to award points. Players may suggest achievements, but the GM makes the awards.

Session voting

Session MVP

  • MVP (Most Valuable Player) — 10 points: At the end of the session, vote for the character who made the most critical and meaningful contributions. Exactly one winner receives 10 points; the GM breaks any tie.

When GM tie-breaking is enabled, the GM determines the winner in a tie.

GM-awarded points
